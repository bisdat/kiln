# Kiln 0.2.0-dev.31.next: the skill respec from linked chests (GitHub #150)

## Players and server admins

- **Respec with Runes from your linked chests.** With a mod that links chests for you (Streamlined Logistics and others,
  when they opt in with `Respec`), unlearning a skill node and the Flame Altar's skill reset take their Runes from those
  chests too, after your bag and your base's Magic chests, all or nothing: either the whole cost is paid or nothing moves.
  Nothing comes from the linked chests when your bag and the Magic chests already cover it. The free reset the game makes
  on its own when a character's skill tree no longer checks out stays free. With a Kiln client the skill tree and the
  altar count the linked Runes, so the unlearn and the reset are offered; a vanilla client still counts only its bag and
  the Magic chests. Thanks to Fuzen777 for the request and the careful write-up (kiln#150).

## Modders

- `CraftSourceOptions.Respec` (off by default): the link also pays the skill respec's Runes. Capability
  `Crafting.RespecSourcesCapability` = `crafting.respec_sources`. Public additions: `Kiln.CraftSourceOptions.Respec`
  (get/init), `const Kiln.Crafting.RespecSourcesCapability`; TestKit `FakeCraftSourceRegistration.Respec`,
  `HarnessCrafting.RespecStock(player)`. Native: `KILN_CRAFT_SOURCE_RESPEC` (8), `KILN_CRAFT_SOURCE_STOCK_RESPEC` (16),
  `KILN_STATIONS_MAX_RESPEC_STOCK`, `kiln.stations.set_respec_stock` appended after `fill_flags`.
- Wire: message 11 on `kiln:craftstock` (`required: false`), the server's respec snapshot; older peers drop it.

## Not yet seen in a real game

- A real client's skill tree and Flame Altar offering the unlearn and the reset from linked Runes.
- A hosting client's prediction world in the moment before the server's respec reaches it.
