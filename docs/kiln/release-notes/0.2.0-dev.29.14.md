# Kiln 0.2.0-dev.29.14

Production stations for mods, with a new SDK (mod API 0.2.6): mods can now keep a base's kilns, smelters, looms and other production stations going, filling them from the base's Magic chests the way the game's own button does, stopping and starting them, picking their recipe and collecting what they made. Everything else is as in 0.2.0-dev.29.13.

## Players

- **Nothing changes until a mod asks.** Without a mod that uses it, your stations work exactly as before.
- **Mods can now run your base's production stations.** A mod can fill a station from your base's Magic chests (the same chests and the same rule as the game's "fill from chests" button: ordinary chests are never touched), stop it now or after the current craft, start it again, choose its recipe and move what it made into your Magic chests. Stopping "now" works like the game's stop button: the running craft starts over and its materials go back into the station; "after the current craft" loses nothing. A Magic chest you set to not accept deposits at the quick-stack station is never chosen to receive a station's output.
- **A production station's craft counts once.** For mods that watch crafting (quest objectives "craft N of …" among them), a station's cycle counted twice, once when it started and once when it ended. It now counts once, when the items are made.

## Mod authors

- **The mod API is 0.2.6.** Everything new is additive: no public member of 0.2.0-dev.29.11 was removed or changed. Use the 0.2.0-dev.29.14 SDK and MDK. Guide: `docs/kiln/services/stations.md`.
- **Bases** (`Bases`, Kiln.Blocks): every base with its altars (each altar's own id), its Magic chests (`Storage`, and `Storage(id, deposit: true)` for those that take deposits), water sources and stock per item, `Bases.Of(entity)`, `Bases.At(position)`, `FindRoomFor`, and `BasesChangedEvent` with Added, Removed, Changed and Kept when altars are added, removed, merged or split. A base's id is its smallest altar id; keep per-base data by altar.
- **Stations** (`Stations`, Kiln.Blocks): `All`, `InBase`, `Nearest(player, workshop, radius)`, `Read` (state, recipe, progress, crafts loaded, inputs, outputs, leftovers, water, `StopPending`), `RecipesOf`, ingredient categories, `Plan`, and the actions `Fill` (up to or add N crafts, reserves with `Keep`, `Exact`, `DryRun`, water, optionally from a named player's bag), `Stop` (`Now` or `AfterCurrentCraft`; a later Resume, Select or Stop withdraws a waiting one), `Resume`, `Select` (refuses recipes no player on the server has unlocked unless `allowLocked`), `Collect` and `CollectToBase`. Actions run in the game's own transaction and answer one step later with a `StationResult` (what moved, what limited it, whether a retry makes sense). Events: `StationStateChangedEvent` (with the mod or player that caused it), `StationCraftedEvent`, `StationPlacedEvent`/`StationRemovedEvent`, and `StationPlayerActionEvent`, a player's button press at a station, which a mod can cancel. Capabilities `stations.read`, `stations.fill`, `stations.control`, `stations.events`. Dedicated servers and games that host their own world; a joined client has no direct access (talk to your mod's server half through Kiln's messages).
- **C:** the new service `kiln.stations` v1 (`kiln_stations.h`, in kiln-items.dll).
- **Behaviour change:** `ItemCraftedEvent` and `KILN_GAMEPLAY_CRAFTED` report an automated station's cycle once, at its end (before, at its start and at its end). A player's crafting is reported as before.

## Checked in a real game

On a game client hosting its own world (Linux, Proton), with Kiln's test mod: a charcoal kiln of the base read with its recipes; select; a plan and a fill from the Magic chests; stop now (the materials back in the station) and resume; stop after the current craft (stopped at the craft's end, nothing lost); collect into a Magic chest; the station's screen agreeing with Kiln's actions and the game's own fill button; a player's stop press seen, cancelled by the mod and, the second time, recorded as that player's; ordinary chests never used (a fill short of materials left the 48 units of its main ingredient in an ordinary chest alone); a Magic chest switched to not accept deposits at the quick-stack station left out of deposit targets. Lab checks on a dedicated server covered the rest (water stations, reserves, exact fills, restarts).

Not yet seen in a real game: a dedicated server with joined players, water stations other than the lab's seedling bed, and recipes with ingredient categories.
