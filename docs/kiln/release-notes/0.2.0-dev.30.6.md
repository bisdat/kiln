# Kiln 0.2.0-dev.30.6

A patch for 0.2.0-dev.30.5. EMF mods built with the current EMF SDK load again, and EMF's block functions work on Kiln, so mods such as Night Raids can read and break blocks. Mod API 0.2.15 adds block operations for Kiln mods (read a block, find a wall, break a box) and Fuzen777's crafting sources: mods can let players craft and dye from linked containers. The save editor shows item, skill and knowledge names in your game's language. The Simplified Chinese translation covers everything new in 30.5.

**Not checked in a real game yet.** Everything passed Kiln's tests, on Windows where it matters. If something looks wrong, tell us on GitHub or Discord with `kiln/logs/launcher.log` or `kiln/logs/kiln.log`.

## Players

- **EMF mods built with the current EMF SDK load again.** Kiln now speaks EMF 0.6.19 (API v47), so mods built with it, for example Night Raids 1.3.0, Beastmaster 1.19.0 and EMF Lua 1.40, pass the version check. Thanks to matenm (#124).
- **EMF mods can read and break blocks.** EMF's block functions (what is at a cell, finding a wall, and queued block breaking) now work on Kiln, including EMF's protection around ruins. Natural ground and unbreakable blocks are never removed. Breaks that would drop items aren't available yet, and mods that ask for them are told so. Breaks near the world's edge work, and a burst of heavy terrain damage no longer switches EMF block breaking off until a restart.
- **The save editor speaks your game's language.** Item, skill and knowledge names come from your installed game's own translations, in all 15 of its languages. Search finds items by the translated name and by the English one. Thanks to MagicFox (XHL).
- **Craft and dye from linked chests, through mods.** Mods can now let you craft from chests they link for you, in another base or outside any base: station, NPC and inventory crafting show and use the counts, and the tinting screen offers the dyes in them. A recipe is paid all or nothing. Thanks to Fuzen777 (#125).
- **Simplified Chinese** covers everything new in 30.5, in the launcher and in the game. Thanks to MagicFox (XHL).
- **Still investigating: AutoDoors and SF Unlimited Flight's glider pitch.** The 30.5 notes said fixes would come in this update. We haven't found the cause yet: in the report, Kiln's glider hook never switched on, and Kiln passes each of AutoDoors' calls through exactly once. A trace from a run where it happens would settle it (the steps are in PepegaJedi's Discord thread). Until then, try SF Unlimited Flight 1.3.0 or switch AutoDoors off for manual doors. Thanks to PepegaJedi for the reports.

## Modders

- **Mod API 0.2.15.** Mods built for 0.2.15 need this Kiln; mods for 0.2.14 and earlier keep working.
- **Block operations (kiln.build).** `Build.BlockAt` reads what is at a cell: air, terrain or a building block, its material and fill, whether the world placed it or a player did (`BlockProvenance`), hardness and health. `Build.FindWall` finds the first wall between two points: buildings by default, with `FindWallFlags.PlayerTerrain` or `Terrain` also player-placed or any terrain. `Build.BreakBox` breaks the building blocks in a box with the game's own crack damage (`BlockBreakOptions`: instant or damage, an optional selection mask, materials, a protected sphere, terrain health scale and healing time), and with `BlockBreakFlags.PlayerTerrain` also player-placed terrain. Natural ground and unbreakable blocks are never removed. The whole box is read back after a break, and the result (`BlockBreakResult`) counts what was damaged, removed, protected or skipped. Terrain is cleared through Kiln's guarded writer. `BlockBreakFlags.Drops` refuses in this release. A full terrain damage map refuses with `BlockRefusal.DamageMapFull`, and nothing is written.
- Capabilities: `build.block_info`, `build.block_metadata`, `build.block_provenance`, `build.find_wall`, `build.break_building`, `build.break_damage`, `build.break_terrain` and `build.break_drops` (off in 30.6), each with its refusal reason.
- **Behaviour change (kiln.build):** an edit that asked for base or zone protection now refuses, instead of going ahead, when that protection can't be checked because the game's base or zone data is unavailable. Explicit `InBases` and `RespectZones = false` choices are unchanged.
- Native: kiln.build gains `block_at`, `find_wall` and `break_box` (table 184 bytes; records `KilnBuildBlockInfo` 64, `KilnBuildBreak` 184, `KilnBuildBreakResult` 136). Callers built against the older table are unchanged.
- **Crafting sources (#125).** `Crafting.AddSource(player, container, CraftSourceOptions)` links a player's container, in any base or in none, as extra crafting stock: `Order` puts it before or after the station outputs, `Filter` (`CraftSourceFilter.Allow` / `Deny`) with `Items` limits what it lends. A source lives as long as its `CraftSourceHandle` or the mod, and is tied to the player's current login (a new registration replaces that player's registrations from earlier logins). A recipe is paid all or nothing inside the game's own recipe transaction. Kiln clients show the counts in station, NPC and inventory crafting. Production stations can't be registered; they lend their outputs through `Stations.SetOutputSource`. Linked sources also feed dyes: the tinting screen lists the dyes in a player's linked sources, and the server takes from the dye bag first, then from the sources, inside the game's own tint step. A server without Kiln offers no source dyes. Capabilities `crafting.sources`, `crafting.source_ui`, `crafting.dye_sources`, `crafting.dye_ui`. Native: kiln.stations gains `add_source`, `remove_source`, `cancel_sources`, `source_stock` and `set_dye_stock` (table 288 bytes). TestKit: `KilnHarness.Crafting` (`Sources`, `Stock`, `Dyes`). Thanks Fuzen777.
- **EMF (kiln-emf).** EmfApi v47 with `spawn_facing`. EMF spawns now check the world a call comes from and EMF's limit of 15 extra components, as EMF does. `block_at`, `find_wall`, `find_wall_ex` (`EMF_FIND_TERRAIN`) and queued `break_blocks` (`EMF_BREAK_WORLD`, `EMF_BREAK_TERRAIN`) run on kiln.build. Kiln clears only the selected cells, where EMF's own clearing can also remove loose terrain just outside the box. Marker bars and drop-producing breaks still report unavailable. EMF parity: 80 full, 19 partial, 45 not yet (docs/kiln/emf-parity.md).
- **Client pass driver (testers).** An optional test-only mod, `mods/tests/client-pass-driver`: `/drive` does a check's in-game action (anvil, forge, well, crafting, provoking an enemy, stepping away) through the game's own requests, so the tester only does what needs the screen. Admins only, never packaged.
